01. Problem Statement

A failed payment isn't necessarily a failed transaction.

The hard problem in payment operations isn't detecting failure. It's deciding what to do next — safely.

TRADITIONAL AUTOMATION

Blind Immediate Retries

IF PAYMENT FAILS → RETRY IMMEDIATELY
  • Flawed Assumption: Blindly assumes all payment failures are transient network blips.
  • Double Debits: Retrying ambiguous gateway timeouts causes duplicate charges.
  • Rate Limiting & Blocks: Repeatedly pounding degraded banking rails triggers bank fraud throttles.
  • Customer Churn: Confusing errors and failed retries push users to abandon checkouts.
IRO DECISION ENGINEERING

Contextual Investigation & Choice

FAILURE → CONTEXT → REASON → POLICY CHECK → SAFE ACTION
IRO analyzes failure context and routes to 1 of 5 distinct operational strategies:
• Retry? — Transient gateway glitch with exponential backoff.
• Switch route? — Primary rail degraded; switch to healthy banking rail.
• Ask customer? — 3DS OTP expired or balance issue; send recovery link.
• Wait? — Gateway timeout; hold for bank reconciliation.
• STOP? — Hard decline, fraud trigger, or merchant limit reached.

How the AI Teammate Works: 6 Bounded Read-Only Tools

The agent investigates payment context with deep precision — but has zero direct write access to move money.

TOOL 01

Payment Context

Amount, currency, merchant ID, customer ID, payment method (UPI, Card, Netbanking).

TOOL 02

Payment Attempts

inspect_payment_attempts

Sequence of prior attempts, gateway latencies, timestamps, and route history.

TOOL 03

Failure History

inspect_failure_history

Error taxonomy classification (Timeout, Socket Drop, Balance, Route Degradation).

TOOL 04

Customer Profile

inspect_customer_profile

Customer risk tier, historical payment success rate, and chargeback signals.

TOOL 05

Merchant Policy

inspect_merchant_policy

Merchant-specific max retry caps, permitted recovery methods, and SLA windows.

TOOL 06

Route Health

inspect_route_health

Real-time banking rail health scores (e.g. HDFC 38% vs ICICI 98.4% uptime).

🔒 HARD ISOLATION BOUNDARY: ZERO WRITE ACCESS
The AI teammate operates in a read-only investigation sandbox. It can inspect context and output structured JSON proposals, but cannot directly invoke payment APIs or mutate database states.
